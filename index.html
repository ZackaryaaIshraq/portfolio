    Courier Dispatch Simulator

    Who should deliver your order? I tested four answers on real city streets.

    Four dispatch strategies raced on the real street networks of Dubai Marina and Istanbul Kadıköy. Optimal matching cut average peak delivery time by about a third. But roughly 93% of that gain came from one simple rule, and it cost the unluckiest customer around 24 extra minutes.
